To deploy, replace the complete /S2A-Pilot/ folder, including .htaccess,
compat/ and companion/. Do not replace index.html alone.
The included QLab gateway is S2A Pilot Bridge 1.3.0.

Purge this folder in the CDN. Avoid long-lived caches for HTML, JS, JSON and
the service worker. Check the displayed version, Edit, Show, both layouts,
FR/EN help, PDF and offline ZIP export. actualiser.html refreshes caches
without erasing IndexedDB projects. Do not clear site data as an update fix.
